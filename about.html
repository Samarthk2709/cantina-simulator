Play your way

First Office is free to play in your browser. Play without an account to keep progress on that browser, or sign in to save across devices and join the public leaderboard. Google sign-in, when available, uses basic identity information only. Game money and rewards are fictional.

Online AI features send game messages and relevant context through DeepInfra to generate dialogue. Avoid sharing secrets or sensitive information in conversations. Read the privacy policy for details and the terms of service for the rules of play.
