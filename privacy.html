Privacy policy

What stays in your browser, what follows your account, and what happens when you talk to Nib.

Effective and last updated: November 4, 2026

First Office is a browser game operated by Samarth Kumbla at first-office-seven.vercel.app. This policy describes the information handled by the game. Contact samarthk@cantina.security with privacy questions or requests.

1. Information the game handles

  • Guest play: game progress, names you choose, character appearance, settings, dialogue, and game memories are saved in your browser’s local storage. Guest progress is not added to the account leaderboard. Online AI features and technical diagnostics can still send information to the server.
  • Accounts: a username, an internal account identifier, and account creation time. Password accounts store a salted password hash rather than the password itself. Signed-in play also stores game progress, customization, agent instructions, dialogue, and game memories on the server so they can follow your account.
  • Google sign-in, when available: Google provides an identity token containing your basic profile information. The game uses your Google account identifier, email address, and profile-picture URL to create or recognize your account. You choose a separate public username. First Office does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or calendars.
  • Technical information: requests expose information such as IP address, browser information, requested pages, and request times to our hosting services. The game also sends performance diagnostics, including frame timings, device/browser performance information, game chapter and day, and the open game window, to help investigate slowdowns.

2. How information is used

Information is used to sign you in, save and restore your game, generate in-game dialogue, run the leaderboard, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and answer support or privacy requests. First Office does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

The public leaderboard shows your chosen username and game statistics, such as reputation, bugs found, in-game earnings, day, and chapter. It does not display your Google email address or profile picture. Choose a username you are comfortable making public.

3. AI dialogue and service providers

When you use online AI features, the game sends the relevant message or instruction, recent dialogue, player-chosen names, remembered facts, and game context to DeepInfra to generate a reply. This can happen during guest play too. Responses and dialogue may be included in your local or cloud save.

Google sign-in identifiers, email addresses, and profile pictures are not included in the game’s AI request fields. Information you type into a game message or choose as a player name may be included. First Office does not use Google sign-in data to train AI models.

Keep game conversations free of passwords, API keys, sensitive personal information, and confidential code.

The hosted game uses Vercel for hosting, Upstash for account and game storage, Google for optional sign-in, and DeepInfra for AI processing. These providers process information needed to supply their services. AI providers and model-specific services may apply their own retention and data-handling rules; see DeepInfra’s data privacy documentation. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably needed to protect the service and its users.

4. Cookies, local storage, and retention

The game uses a first-party session cookie to keep you signed in, normally for up to 30 days, and browser storage for saves and preferences. Clearing this site’s browser data removes local saves and preferences and may sign you out; it does not delete server-side account data. Google and hosting providers may use their own cookies or storage when providing their services.

Account records and cloud saves currently have no automatic expiry and remain stored until removed. Diagnostic records are rotated by volume rather than a fixed number of days. Service providers may retain operational logs or backups under their own policies. We use access controls and protected connections, but no online service can guarantee complete security. Hosted processing may take place outside your country.

5. Your choices and requests

You can play without an account, sign out, clear your browser’s site data, or remove First Office’s connection from your Google Account connections. Removing the Google connection does not itself delete an existing First Office account or cloud save.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information, cloud saves, or your leaderboard entry, email samarthk@cantina.security with your username and request. We may need to verify ownership before acting. Deletion is handled by the operator; there is currently no self-service account-deletion button. Limited records may need to remain for security or legal reasons, and provider backups may persist under their retention schedules. Applicable privacy rights depend on where you live.

6. Children and policy changes

First Office is intended for people aged 13 or older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

Changes to data practices will be reflected here with a new update date. Material changes will also be brought to players’ attention in the game before they take effect.
